How to Play

🍬
Match 3 Candies
Swap adjacent candies by clicking or swiping horizontally or vertically to line up 3 of the same color.
⚡
Striped Candy (Match 4)
Match 4 candies in a row. When triggered, it blasts an entire row or column of candies!
🎁
Wrapped Candy (L or T Shape)
Match 5 in an L or T formation. Triggers a double 3x3 explosive candy blast!
🌈
Color Bomb (Match 5)
Match 5 in a straight line. Swap it with any candy to clear all candies of that color from the board!
💥
Special Combos
Swap two special candies together for massive cross-screen combos (e.g. Color Bomb + Striped)!
